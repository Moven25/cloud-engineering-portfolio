## Approved training protocol (2026-11-27)

This protocol supersedes earlier daily lesson routines. Target five hours/day, five days/week when feasible; progress by competency, not calendar. A+ coursework only; Security+ first certification; AWS primary.

For every video: prior interactive flashcard review (10–15 min); watch lesson; concept and vocabulary discussion (5–10 min); new interactive flashcards (5–10 min); knowledge and oral assessment (5–10 min); practical activity or simulated ticket (10–30 min); interview-style explanation (5–10 min); document evidence and competency. End each section with cumulative exam, integrated lab, independent troubleshooting and portfolio review. Review at 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Terminology exams at each section end, every two sections cumulatively, and course end. Test acronym, definition, purpose and usage. Minimum knowledge target 85%, terminology 90%, plus independent practical competency; remediate and reassess gaps. Keep individual scores private.

Professional development: integrate 10–15 minutes daily and one weekly simulated meeting or presentation. Practice executive communication, business acumen, critical thinking, presentation, stakeholder management, ownership and emotional intelligence. Role-play CTO and challenging stakeholders; explain costs, risks, alternatives and tradeoffs. Increase independence and difficulty gradually.

Review employer requirements monthly to prioritize advanced labs: AWS networking, IAM/security, Terraform, Python, CI/CD, containers, Kubernetes, observability, incident recovery, FinOps and secure AI/RAG applications. Include a non-logistics capstone. These are planned, not completed; preserve existing assignment IDs and evidence. The intensive 12–18 month training estimate is provisional and distinct from the longer career development horizon.
