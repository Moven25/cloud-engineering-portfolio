# Content and portfolio standards

For each verified lab, consider a post: problem, method, result, lesson, business relevance, project link. Use your own voice; avoid invented metrics and generic claims. Label simulations, redact sensitive information and disclose assistance accurately.

Featured order: recruiter landing page, strongest relevant completed case study, architecture project, financial case study when available. No empty project promoted as finished.

Weekly review: choose one or two genuine learning insights; write a draft; verify evidence; manually publish. No automated posting.

Private tracker fields: date, company, role, job URL, relevant project, application status, contact, follow-up date, response, interview outcome. Keep actual entries outside public repository.
