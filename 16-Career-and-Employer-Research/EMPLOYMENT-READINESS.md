# Employment readiness gates
Approved 2026-11-25. Baseline not assessed. Private scores and feedback stay private.
| Gate | Practical scope |
|---|---|
| A — Tier 1 | Hardware, Windows, basic networking, customer communication, ticket resolution |
| B — Tier 2 | Independent Windows troubleshooting, AD/GPO, M365, permissions, VPN, DNS/DHCP, PowerShell |
| C — Infrastructure | Servers, virtualization, network services, backups, monitoring, automation |
| D — Cloud | AWS, Linux, Terraform, IAM, networking, deployment, monitoring, recovery, cost and architecture |

Every 30 days review evidence and choose appropriate gate; do not wait until summer if ready. Use existing assessment standards: practical 30%, independent troubleshooting 25%, understanding 20%, communication/documentation 15%, security/business judgment 10%. Target 80% overall, level 3 practical ability and level 4 core troubleshooting, mandatory safety, evidence integrity and appropriate escalation. Repeat unfamiliar tasks to confirm consistency. No score alone guarantees employability. Compare actual role requirements; seek external feedback where feasible.
Comprehensive assessment across sessions: knowledge 45 min, independent lab 60 min, five tickets 90 min, interview 45 min, customer communication 20 min, documentation 20 min. Allow technical documentation, no step-by-step coaching. Record assistance and gaps. Gate B is aspirational for first job; Gate A can justify suitable entry roles. Gate C/D may support direct cloud transition without Tier 3 help desk. Review cloud readiness after approximately six months of employment or sooner based on evidence.

## Workplace readiness overlay
Use [workplace protocol](../WORKPLACE-SIMULATION-PROTOCOL.md) at each gate: customer communication, prioritization, escalation, inherited systems, review, safe changes and handoff as appropriate. The 80% overall readiness rubric is distinct from 85% knowledge and 90% terminology targets. Simulations do not waive employer experience requirements.
