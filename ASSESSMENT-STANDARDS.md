# Assessment standards

0 not introduced; 1 recognizes; 2 explains unaided; 3 performs with limited assistance; 4 independently troubleshoots unfamiliar cases; 5 evaluates alternatives and defends decisions.

Weights: practical execution 30%, independent troubleshooting 25%, understanding 20%, documentation/communication 15%, business/security judgment 10%. Target 85% for knowledge assessments and 90% for terminology assessments; mandatory safety, security and evidence integrity gates. Adapt weights for beginner tasks before grading. Use spaced retrieval, reassess gaps with new scenarios, distinguish guided from independent. Keep individual grades, feedback, raw notes and exam questions outside public GitHub, locally or in separately access-controlled storage. No private repository created.

Use daily interactive flashcards, section-end terminology reviews, cumulative terminology exams every two sections and course-end review. Review at 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Require practical competency independently of scores. Include daily professional communication practice and weekly stakeholder simulations; assess clarity, judgment, ownership and tradeoff explanations.
