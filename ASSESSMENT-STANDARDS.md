# Assessment standards

0 not introduced; 1 recognizes; 2 explains unaided; 3 performs with limited assistance; 4 independently troubleshoots unfamiliar cases; 5 evaluates alternatives and defends decisions.

Weights: practical execution 30%, independent troubleshooting 25%, understanding 20%, documentation/communication 15%, business/security judgment 10%. Target 80% for substantial assessments; mandatory safety, security and evidence integrity gates. Adapt weights for beginner tasks before grading. Use spaced retrieval, reassess gaps with new scenarios, distinguish guided from independent. Keep individual grades, feedback, raw notes and exam questions outside public GitHub, locally or in separately access-controlled storage. No private repository created.
