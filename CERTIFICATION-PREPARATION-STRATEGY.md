# Certification preparation strategy
Planned order: Security+ first; AWS Solutions Architect Associate; HashiCorp Terraform Associate; AWS CloudOps Engineer Associate; AWS Machine Learning Engineer Associate. Recheck current exam availability, objectives, costs and employer relevance before registration; AI exam choice may change as offerings evolve. A+, Network+, AZ-900, Cloud Practitioner and AI Practitioner knowledge only. FinOps Practitioner and professional-level AWS credentials deferred; no mandatory additional exams for first employment.
Map current official exam objectives to lessons and labs, track gaps, use original practice questions and official resources, then fresh timed comprehensive assessments. Target consistent 90% on fresh representative practice exams plus independent practical competence; this is a coaching target, not an official passing score or guarantee. Preserve 85% knowledge and 90% terminology standards for regular training. No exam dumps, copyrighted question banks in public repo or claims of certification before passing. No purchases or bookings without learner approval. Apply for suitable jobs when ready, not after all five exams.
